About Us

Equator Surveys Limited is a fully registered surveying and mapping firm offering professional services in land surveying, GIS, hydrography, and project management. We are dedicated to accuracy, innovation, and client satisfaction in every project we undertake.

Our Vision

To be Kenya’s most trusted and innovative surveying and geospatial solutions provider, delivering excellence in every project.

Our Mission

To provide accurate, efficient, and reliable surveying and mapping solutions that empower development, planning, and environmental sustainability.
